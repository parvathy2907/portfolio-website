ATT&CK → D3FEND
Final-year research · In progress

DEFENSIQ: Ransomware Defense Recommendation Engine

Runs about 3,000 ransomware samples through Cuckoo Sandbox, scores each behaviour for impact, and maps it through MITRE ATT&CK to candidate D3FEND countermeasures. A Cooperative Co-Evolutionary Algorithm then searches for the defense set that leaves the least residual severity, benchmarked against a greedy baseline with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.

Offer · counter · deal
Multi-agent simulation · AI

Dynamic Price Wars: Perishable Inventory Liquidation

A multi-agent Python simulation where a distributor negotiates the sale of perishable stock with a premium grocer and a budget retailer, each with its own budget, storage capacity, willingness to pay and freshness requirement. As shelf life runs down, the distributor's reservation price falls, and agents trade opening offers, counteroffers, acceptances and walk-aways.

  • Optional AI-driven negotiation through a local LLM (Ollama), with Pydantic-validated decisions and a rule-based fallback.
  • Every deal is checked against market constraints, and the simulation tracks revenue, units sold, failed negotiations and expired inventory.
  • Interactive Streamlit dashboard with Plotly charts, backed by a pytest suite covering agents, pricing, negotiation and end-to-end runs.
